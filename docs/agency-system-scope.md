## Non-negotiable delivery behavior

- Preserve Google Drive folder names and nested hierarchy. Do not flatten the client file browser.
- Connect the client-facing `Client Delivery Files` folder, not the agency's entire internal client folder.
- Folder assignment and adding a descendant do not grant review or download permission.
- Review, client approval, payment eligibility, final release and public publication are independent states.
- Agency users explicitly send selected work for review and explicitly release final files.
- Review supports version history, comments, requests for revisions and formal decisions by authorized client approvers.
- Concept rounds support comparing three or more options, selecting a direction, requesting revisions, and rejecting all with feedback. Selecting a direction is not final approval.
- Google Docs remain internal working documents; client reviews should use versioned snapshots with page/section comments. Never claim visible content cannot be captured.
- Keep original/source assets inaccessible during review. A preview must not accidentally unlock the original/download path.
- Final files, site publication/transfer, hosting/admin handoff, and rights-release status remain blocked until required payments are paid in full. A payment plan does not waive this gate.
- Full payment enables agency release; it does not automatically release all files.
- Super Admin overrides require a reason and an audit event. Ordinary corporate client admins are not agency Super Admins.
- Before initial payment, production is not activated. After initial payment, onboarding/reviews can proceed. Plans normally begin with at least 50%, configurable per agreement.
- Past-due installments, grace periods, fees, notices, pause state, suspension eligibility after 30+ days, and restoration must be tracked. Suspension/restoration are manual authorized actions, never automatic deletion.
- Scope any interim manual payment confirmation clearly; it is not a substitute for verified processor payment integration.

## Complete commercial workflow

- Pipeline: lead sources and source detail, UTM/human attribution, qualification, owner, follow-up/discovery dates, opportunity lifecycle, linkage to requests/proposals/projects.
- Start a Project: progressive company/needs/situation/timing/investment intake, optional materials, save-and-return where appropriate. Reuse existing Services Hub rather than duplicating intake blindly.
- Services: descriptions, scope, timelines, pricing types/amounts, deposit/payment choices, visibility, internal notes; role-controlled pricing changes with immutable history and effective dates.
- Packages: reusable services/deliverables, optional add-ons, configurable tiers and custom scope.
- Proposals: drafts/internal review, send/view, selected tier, exact scope/price snapshots, issue/expiry dates, refresh into a new version, never overwrite sent/signed history.
- Signature: legal name, authority, explicit electronic consent, immutable agreement/version/terms and acceptance evidence.
- Payments: exact signed scope and due-now amount; hosted checkout; verified webhooks and idempotency; deposits, installments, recurring/manual/no-payment-due options; project activation after verified initial payment or audited override.
- QuickBooks remains accounting. Do not build a general ledger or replace ClickUp.
- Revenue reporting: leads, qualification, sent/signed value, collected payments, outstanding follow-ups/expiry, conversion rates, sources/services/packages/industry, date and owner filters; use defined denominators, not invented precision.

## Project delivery and integrations

- Accepted scope becomes project/deliverables without retyping.
- Conditional intake with save-and-return, client asset submission, versioned deliverables/reviews, release bundles, decision authority and audit history.
- ClickUp remains internal production management. Start with references; integrate explicit project/review/revision/release events with defined ownership and retry/idempotency behavior. ClickUp completion never authorizes release.
- Ignite OS launch-team mapping, client-facing activation status and internal activation workflow reuse confirmed project scope.

## Portfolio publishing

- Release creates a private review candidate except applicable Never Public/Private Proof Only cases; no assumption of public permission.
- Admin portfolio navigation, filtered saved views, completeness checks, separate record and destination statuses.
- Project facts/service taxonomy, industry context (not primary public gallery filter), public naming/logo/visual/guideline/testimonial permission evidence, anonymous/private/never-public handling.
- Public-safe image selection, featured/hero/social roles, crop/focal point, ordering, export filenames, alt text/captions and rights controls.
- Separate TBR authority case study and concise Freedom Foundry proof page: separate titles/copy/metadata/CTAs/indexing/canonical/social images with TBR attribution.
- Private SEO Reference Library supports the brief's document formats and Drive references; AI suggestions use approved inputs only and never fabricate facts or publish.
- Preview cards/pages/search snippets/social images; explicit approve/schedule/publish/unpublish/archive actions with audit history.
- Public work libraries filter by work/service type, stable crawlable pages, metadata and sitemap behavior. Actual destination hosting/routing must be verified before configuring.
- Private proof links: optional password/expiry/verified recipient restriction, view analytics, revocation, noindex and no sitemap/gallery inclusion. Noindex is not access control.
- Do not expose raw Drive URLs, original/source files, contracts, credentials, internal notes, unapproved/confidential assets or full private guideline books publicly.
- Later enhancements in the brief remain scope: AI asset/gallery suggestions, companion content and social/email drafts, proposal proof collections, analytics/Search Console, permission requests, related work and service/industry collections.
