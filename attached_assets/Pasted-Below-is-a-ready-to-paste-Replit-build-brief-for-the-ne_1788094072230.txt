Below is a ready-to-paste **Replit build brief** for the new “Start a Project” / pricing-guidance flow in Freedom Foundry. It includes the user experience, questionnaire logic, recommended service menu, starting investment ranges, the separate referral-network disclaimer, and a Brand Coaching offer.

I recommend positioning brand coaching a little above generic business coaching because it combines business direction with your specialized expertise in positioning, messaging, offer clarity, and brand-led growth. Current small-business and marketing coaching references commonly place experienced coaching around $150–$500 per hour, while specialist or executive-level work can run higher; a $300–$450 strategic session and $1,250–$2,500+ monthly coaching structure would be credible starting territory for your experience and positioning. ## Recommended pricing approach

Your instinct is right: brand coaching should not be priced like a low-cost “pick my brain” call. You are not offering generic motivation or accountability. You are offering senior-level brand direction, positioning experience, messaging judgment, creative-business insight, and access to the broader Freedom Foundry ecosystem.

### Brand coaching starting point

| Offer | Suggested launch pricing | Why it works |
|---|---:|---|
| **90-minute Brand Coaching Session** | $350 | Accessible enough to become a first paid step; premium enough to protect your expertise and preparation |
| **Half-day Brand Clarity Intensive** | $750–$1,250 | Good for a deeper single-day strategic reset; can lead into a roadmap or larger project |
| **Brand Coaching Partnership** | Starting at $1,250/month | A reasonable 3-month entry point for two structured sessions monthly plus limited portal-based support |
| **Brand Advisory Partnership** | Starting at $2,500/month | Appropriate for established founders or teams who need closer strategic counsel, priority review, and higher-touch direction |
| **Fractional Brand Director** | Custom scope; generally start conversations at $3,500–$5,000+/month | Reserve for clients needing consistent leadership across brand, campaigns, marketing decisions, vendors, and team direction |

For context—not as a reason to price yourself by competitors—business and marketing coaching often falls around $150–$500 per hour, while executive coaching and experienced specialist advisory work can be materially higher. Your $350/90-minute session begins at about $233 per live coaching hour before preparation and follow-up, which is not overreaching for a specialist brand strategist; it is actually a sensible entry offer. ## A few implementation notes

### Keep the price ranges editable

Ask Replit to put all pricing, descriptions, included items, service names, and routing rules in an **admin-managed configuration area**, not hard-coded throughout the app.

You should be able to update:

- Starting prices and investment ranges.
- Which services are actively offered.
- Which questionnaire answers route to each service.
- Results-page language.
- The independent-expert disclaimer.
- Academy resource recommendations.
- GoHighLevel or other tool offers.
- Available Brand Coaching session types.
- Whether a service is shown publicly, only inside the member portal, or only when recommended.

### Start with fewer visible services

The internal catalog can hold everything, but the initial results screen should generally show no more than:

- One primary recommendation.
- One secondary option.
- One lower-investment alternative.
- One “talk with the team” option.

For example:

> **Primary recommendation:** Brand Strategy and Messaging — typical planning range $4,500–$9,500.  
> **A lighter first step:** Brand Coaching Session — $350 for 90 minutes.  
> **Build capacity first:** Recommended Academy pathway and resources.  
> **Need a team to own execution?** Request a project conversation.

That gives people choices without creating decision fatigue.

### Keep partner pricing completely separate

The independent-professional note belongs directly beneath your own service/pricing section, exactly as you described. The user should understand:

- You can recommend a fractional CFO, CMO, business planner, legal professional, technology expert, or specialist.
- You have vetted them for fit and quality.
- You may facilitate a warm introduction.
- Their contracts and fees are direct with them.
- Their fee is not included in a Freedom Foundry Studio project unless you explicitly scope it that way.
