# Freedom Foundry Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Freedom Foundry, a product of The Brand Revivalist, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information shared through the platform.

## Information collected

Freedom Foundry may collect the following categories of information:

- Account information, such as name, email address, login credentials, and profile details.
- Usage information, such as feature activity, navigation behavior, settings changes, and interaction logs.
- Content you choose to upload, create, save, or submit within the platform.
- Billing or subscription-related information processed through third-party payment providers.
- Integration data received from connected third-party services, including accounting, CRM, marketing, automation, and business-platform providers.

## How information is used

Information may be used to:

- Provide, operate, maintain, and improve Freedom Foundry.
- Personalize dashboards, content, settings, and user experiences.
- Support integrations with third-party services such as QuickBooks and GoHighLevel.
- Authenticate users, secure accounts, and prevent fraud or misuse.
- Communicate about account activity, updates, support matters, and service-related notices.
- Process transactions, subscriptions, or service requests.
- Analyze performance, troubleshoot issues, and improve platform functionality.

## QuickBooks and other integrations

If you choose to connect QuickBooks, GoHighLevel, or any other third-party service, Freedom Foundry may access and process only the data necessary to provide the requested integration features.

Depending on the integration, this may include customer records, invoices, payments, transaction summaries, contact data, workflow-related data, account identifiers, or similar business information made available through the third-party provider.

Freedom Foundry does not use connected-service data for unrelated purposes beyond operating requested features, improving platform functionality, meeting legal obligations, or enforcing platform terms.

## Sharing of information

Information may be shared only in the following circumstances:

- With service providers and vendors that help operate the platform.
- With integration partners when you choose to connect external services.
- When required by law, regulation, court order, or legal process.
- To protect the rights, safety, security, or property of Freedom Foundry, its users, or others.
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, sale of assets, or similar business transaction.

Freedom Foundry does not sell your personal information.

## Data retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the platform, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

## Data security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to help protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## Your choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

- Access, update, or correct certain personal information.
- Request deletion of certain personal information.
- Disconnect third-party integrations.
- Opt out of certain non-essential communications.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information below.

## Third-party services

Freedom Foundry may link to or integrate with third-party services that operate under their own privacy policies and terms. The Brand Revivalist is not responsible for the privacy or security practices of third-party platforms.

## Children's privacy

Freedom Foundry is not directed to children under 13, and it is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The revised version becomes effective when posted with an updated effective date.
