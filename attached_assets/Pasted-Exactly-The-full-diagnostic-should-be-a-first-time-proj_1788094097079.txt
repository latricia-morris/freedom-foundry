Exactly. The full diagnostic should be a **first-time project-planning experience**, not a hurdle clients must clear every time they need help. Once someone has worked with you—or has an established Brand Portal—the platform should recognize them, retain their context, and make future requests feel like tapping an informed partner on the shoulder.

That continuity is one of Freedom Foundry’s strongest differentiators: it remembers the brand, the work already completed, the assets available, the team involved, past decisions, and the client’s evolving goals.

## Updated workflow model

Use three distinct request pathways:

| User type | Experience | Required effort |
|---|---|---|
| **New visitor or first-time client** | Full “Find My Best Starting Point” diagnostic | Approximately 5–7 guided questions plus conditional scope questions |
| **Existing member / previous client** | Quick project request using existing Brand Portal information | Choose request type, desired outcome, timeline, and any changes |
| **Returning after 12+ months** | Short “Business Update” check-in before a new request | 3–5 update questions, then the quick project request |

This means the long-form planner becomes a strategic onboarding and qualification tool—not an annoying recurring intake form.

## The client experience

The key message is: **the Brand Portal compounds in value over time.**

A first-time visitor needs to be guided through their stage, needs, assets, and priorities. A returning client should feel the payoff of having worked with you before:

> “We already know your brand. What are you building now?”

That is a profoundly better experience than requiring them to explain their business, find their logo, repeat their audience, and reconstruct prior project details every time they need a website update, campaign, new offer, coaching, automation, or design support.

## Keep “year or more” flexible

The 12-month trigger is smart, but I would make it configurable in admin settings—perhaps defaulting to 12 months. Some past clients may return after 8–10 months with a business that has changed dramatically; others may return after 18 months and need only a small update.

Include this configuration field:

```text
BUSINESS UPDATE THRESHOLD:
Default: 12 months
Admin editable options: 6 months / 9 months / 12 months / 18 months / 24 months
```

The app should use the reminder as a helpful **context refresh**, not a gatekeeping device.
