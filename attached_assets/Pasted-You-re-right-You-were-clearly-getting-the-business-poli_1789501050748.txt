## The exact brief language for Replit

Paste this under **Payment + Release Rules**:

> **Work-product and release rule:** Until the client’s account is paid in full, all work remains agency work product. Clients may access review-stage materials only for feedback and approval. They do not receive downloadable final files, editable/source files, final asset bundles, website publication, site transfer, hosting/admin access, or final usage/ownership rights until the account is paid in full.  
>
> **Final-release gate:** The system must block final asset release, final-file downloads, website publishing, website transfer, hosting/admin handoff, and final rights-release status whenever any required project payment remains outstanding. Super Admin may override any block, but the system must require an internal reason and create an audit-log entry.  
>
> **Payment-plan rule:** A payment plan changes when amounts are due; it does not allow final release before the balance is paid. Projects with a payment plan should normally begin with at least a 50% initial payment. The final installment must clear before final deliverables, publishing, transfer, or release.  
>
> **Delinquency rule:** The system must track each installment’s due date, grace period, days overdue, late fee, outstanding balance, reminders, pause status, and hosted-site suspension eligibility. For eligible agency-hosted sites, an account more than 30 days overdue may be marked `Suspension Eligible`; the Admin must manually initiate notice, suspension, restoration, or override actions.  
>
> **Restoration rule:** A suspended hosted site cannot be republished until all required past-due payments, approved late charges, and any configured restoration fee are paid or manually waived by Super Admin.
