# Integrations

Manage the tools that connect Freedom Foundry to the rest of your business ecosystem.

## Available integrations

### QuickBooks

Use the QuickBooks integration to support accounting visibility, reporting workflows, and connected financial operations.

**Primary actions:**
- Connect QuickBooks
- Reconnect QuickBooks
- Disconnect QuickBooks
- View connection status

**Suggested status labels:**
- Connected
- Not connected
- Needs reconnect
- Error

### GoHighLevel

Use the GoHighLevel integration to support CRM, automation, contact management, campaign coordination, and operational workflows.

**Primary actions:**
- Connect GoHighLevel
- Reconnect GoHighLevel
- Disconnect GoHighLevel
- View connection status

**Suggested status labels:**
- Coming soon
- Connected
- Not connected
- Needs reconnect
