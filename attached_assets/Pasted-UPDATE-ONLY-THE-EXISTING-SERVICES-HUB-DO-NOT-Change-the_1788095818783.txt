PROJECT STARTING POINT FLOW
--------------------------------------------------

THIS FLOW IS FOR:
- New members.
- Members who have not previously requested a project.
- Members who explicitly choose to reassess their needs.

THIS FLOW IS NOT REQUIRED FOR:
- Existing/recent clients with existing Brand Portal context.
- Returning clients who simply need help with a new update or next project.

FLOW TITLE:
Find My Starting Point

FLOW INTRO:
Answer a few quick questions so we can understand what you are trying to accomplish and recommend the most appropriate next step. This is a planning tool—not a final quote.

KEEP THIS SHORT:
Use 5 core questions maximum, with optional conditional questions only when necessary.

QUESTION 1:
What do you need help with most right now?
- Branding or rebranding
- Website or digital experience
- Design, collateral, campaign, or launch assets
- Brand guidance or strategic direction
- SaaS, CRM, automation, or client journey
- Help connecting with a trusted specialist
- I am not sure

QUESTION 2:
What type of business are you building?
- Service business
- Product business
- Hybrid business: services, products, courses, membership, media, or multiple offers
- Organization, franchise, nonprofit, or multi-location business
- I am still figuring it out

QUESTION 3:
What best describes your current situation?
- I am building a new brand or business foundation
- I need a focused update or refresh
- My business has changed and I likely need a rebrand
- I am launching something new
- I have a specific project in mind
- I am not sure

QUESTION 4:
How hands-on do you need the support to be?
- I need direction and can handle much of the implementation
- I need help with selected parts
- I need a team to lead the work
- I am not sure

QUESTION 5:
When do you hope to begin?
- As soon as possible
- Within 30 days
- Within 1–3 months
- Planning ahead
- Not sure

OPTIONAL QUESTION:
What are you trying to accomplish?
Use a short text field.

DO NOT ASK:
- Full detailed persona questions.
- Full asset inventory.
- Long budget forms.
- Full business-plan questions.
- Every question already stored in the Brand Portal.

RESULT:
Show a concise result card, not a long report.

EXAMPLE:
Likely starting point: Strategic Brand System conversation

Why:
Based on your business type, current stage, and goal, your next move may involve clarifying your market position, message, visual identity, and the practical tools needed to activate the brand.

What may be involved:
- Strategic brand work
- Market and audience intelligence
- Messaging and visual identity
- Website, launch, campaign, or activation planning as relevant

Final scope is determined after review. This is not a quote.

ACTIONS:
- Start a Conversation
- Explore Brand Guidance
- Browse Relevant Resources
- Save for Later

Do not show public pricing amounts until the owner enters approved pricing into admin settings.

RETURNING CLIENT WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------

User has a previous project request, past project, or current Brand Portal context
AND:
Their last project/context update is less than 12 months old

THEN:
Do NOT show the full Project Starting Point flow.

Show a simple quick request form.

TITLE:
What do you need help with now?

SUBHEAD:
We already have context for your brand and business. Tell us what you are working on now, and we will begin with what is already in your Brand Portal.

QUESTIONS:
1. What do you need help with?
2. What are you trying to accomplish?
3. What has changed since we last worked together? Optional.
4. When would you like to begin?
5. What existing Brand Portal assets should we review? Optional asset selector/upload.

CTA:
Send My Request

IF:
It has been 12 months or more since the user’s last request, project completion, or Business Snapshot update

THEN:
Show a short Business Update before the quick request.

BUSINESS UPDATE TITLE:
A quick update before we begin

BUSINESS UPDATE COPY:
Your business may have evolved since we last worked together. A few quick updates will help us support the business you have now—not the version of it from a year ago.

ASK ONLY:
1. What has changed?
   - Audience
   - Offers/products/services
   - Business direction
   - Team/leadership
   - Technology/website/CRM
   - Expansion/locations
   - No major changes
   - Other
2. What is your most important goal now?
3. Would you like to update any brand assets, offers, website links, or team information?
4. Continue to quick request form.

DO NOT:
- Tell users their data “expired.”
- Require them to start over.
- Block a project request because they do not complete a full reassessment.
