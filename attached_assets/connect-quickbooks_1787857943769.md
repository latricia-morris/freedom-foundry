# Connect QuickBooks

Connect your QuickBooks account to Freedom Foundry to streamline visibility across your business operations.

## What this connection can support

Depending on the features enabled in your account, connecting QuickBooks may allow Freedom Foundry to:

- View accounting data and summary metrics.
- Pull customer, invoice, payment, expense, and transaction information.
- Support reporting, dashboard visibility, and connected workflows.
- Improve financial organization across your business ecosystem.

## Before you connect

Make sure you:

- Have permission to access the QuickBooks company you want to connect.
- Understand that you are authorizing Freedom Foundry to access approved QuickBooks data based on the permissions granted during connection.
- Review the Privacy Policy and End-User License Agreement before connecting.
