# QuickBooks Disconnected

Your QuickBooks connection has been disconnected from Freedom Foundry.

## What this means

- Freedom Foundry should no longer be able to access newly authorized QuickBooks data through this connection.
- Any features that rely on the QuickBooks integration may stop working until the connection is restored.
- Historical information already processed inside the platform may remain available according to your account settings, legal obligations, and retention practices.

## Next steps

If this disconnect was intentional, no further action is required.

If this disconnect was accidental or you want to restore access, reconnect your account using the QuickBooks connection page.

**Button label suggestion:** Reconnect QuickBooks

## Need help?

If you did not expect this change or believe the disconnect happened in error, contact support.
