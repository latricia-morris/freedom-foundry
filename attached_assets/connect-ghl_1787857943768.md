# Connect GoHighLevel

Connect your GoHighLevel account to Freedom Foundry to support CRM alignment, automation visibility, and connected business workflows.

## What this connection can support

Depending on enabled features, connecting GoHighLevel may allow Freedom Foundry to:

- Access approved CRM and contact data.
- Support campaign, automation, or pipeline-related workflows.
- Help coordinate reporting and operational visibility across connected systems.
- Trigger or support future workflow automation inside the platform.

## Before you connect

Make sure you:

- Have authorization to connect the GoHighLevel account or sub-account.
- Understand that provider permissions are granted during the secure authorization flow.
- Review the platform Privacy Policy and End-User License Agreement.
